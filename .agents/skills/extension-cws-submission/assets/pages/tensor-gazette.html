Fine-tuning small LLMs with LoRA

Train a few million parameters instead of billions, and leave the base model untouched.

Juno Park · Oct 14, 2026 · 7 min read

Full fine-tuning updates every weight in a model. Low-rank adaptation takes another route: it freezes the pretrained weights and learns a small update for chosen layers, written as the product of two thin matrices, B and A. With a rank of 8 or 16, the trainable part is often well under one percent of the model.

Because the base model never changes, one copy can serve many tasks. Each task keeps only its small adapter, loaded at inference time or merged back into the weights.

Figure 1. Only the update ΔW = BA is trained; W stays frozen.
from peft import LoraConfig, get_peft_model

config = LoraConfig(
    r=16,
    lora_alpha=32,
    target_modules=["q_proj", "v_proj"],
    lora_dropout=0.05,
    task_type="CAUSAL_LM",
)
model = get_peft_model(base_model, config)
model.print_trainable_parameters()
