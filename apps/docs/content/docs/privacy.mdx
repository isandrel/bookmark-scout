## In short

- **No account and no server.** Bookmark Scout runs inside your browser. The developer receives nothing from the extension.
- **No telemetry.** No analytics, crash reporting, or ads in the extension.
- **AI is off until you turn it on**, and then requests go straight from your browser to the provider you chose.

## What the extension reads

- Your bookmarks, to show, search, edit, move, delete, import, and export them.
- The title and URL of the active tab, so you can save the page you are on.
- Site icons from the browser's own icon cache, in Chrome and Edge.

It does not read the content of the pages you visit.

## What it stores

| Data | Where | Synced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings, including whether AI is on and which provider and model you chose | Browser sync storage | Yes, through your browser account when browser sync is on |
| Bookmark table layout in the manager | Browser sync storage | Same as settings |
| AI provider settings: API key, Base URL, extra headers | Local storage on this device | No |
| Tags, summaries, saved searches, recent folders, recent searches | Local storage on this device | No |

Scan results from the Tools sidebar are kept only while their dialog is open. Uninstalling the extension removes what it stored; your bookmarks stay in the browser.

## When data leaves your browser

Only when you use one of these, and only to the place named:

| Feature | Goes to | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI folder recommendations | The AI provider you configured | The current page's title and URL, and your folder names |
| Auto-Tagging, Content Summarizer, AI Folder Reorganization | The AI provider you configured | Titles, URLs, and folder paths of the bookmarks in scope. No page content |
| Check Dead Links, Metadata Fetcher | Each bookmarked website | A request for the page, without cookies, after you allow website access |

A provider running on your own computer, such as Ollama, keeps AI requests on your machine. Exports, including **AI Context Packer**, are saved as files on your computer and uploaded nowhere; a privacy review can redact sensitive values first. The exact data each AI tool sends is listed in [Set up AI tools](/guides/ai-tools#what-each-tool-sends), and each permission is explained in [Permissions](/permissions).

## This docs site

The docs are a static site with no analytics, no accounts, and no cookies of their own. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google Fonts. The privacy policy names the hosting provider.
