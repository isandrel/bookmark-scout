## What you can do with it

- **Save to the right folder**: type part of a folder's name in the popup or side panel, even a folder deep in the tree or a path such as `work/react`, then press `↓` and `Enter`, or click the folder, to save the current page there. With AI turned on, you can also ask for a folder suggestion. See [Save and organize](/guides/save-and-organize).
- **Save without opening anything**: press `Alt+Shift+S` on any page to save it to the folder that matches it best. A small notification in the page says where it went, with **Undo** and other folders to move it to. See [Save with a keyboard shortcut](/guides/save-and-organize#quick-save).
- **Find**: search every bookmark by title or URL from the toolbar popup or the side panel, and keep named searches in the bookmarks manager. See [Search and saved searches](/guides/search).
- **Organize**: save links from the right-click menu, and use a bookmarks manager that works like Chrome's, with selection, right-click menus, cut, copy, and paste, drag and drop, and undo and redo with `Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Y`. See [Save and organize](/guides/save-and-organize).
- **Clean up**: remove duplicate bookmarks, strip tracking parameters, and find and repair dead links. See [Clean up duplicates and tracking URLs](/guides/clean-up) and [Find and repair dead links](/guides/dead-links).
- **Move your library**: export to HTML, JSON, Markdown, or CSV, and import HTML or JSON after a preview. See [Import and export](/guides/import-export).
- **Use AI, only if you want to**: AI tools are off by default. When you turn them on, you pick the provider and bring your own key. **Ask AI** answers questions about your bookmarks, and changes them only after you approve its plan. See [Set up AI tools](/guides/ai-tools).
- **Work with local AI agents**: let AI agents on your computer, such as Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor, look up your bookmarks, and approve each change they ask for in the browser. Off by default. See [Use with local AI agents](/guides/local-agents).

## Where your data goes

Bookmark Scout has no account and no server of its own. Your bookmarks stay in your browser. Data leaves the browser only when you use a feature that needs the network, such as checking dead links or an AI tool you turned on, and then it goes directly to the website or provider you chose. Local AI agents, when you allow them, get what they look up through a program on your computer. Read the [privacy summary](/privacy).

## Browsers

Chrome is the primary target. Edge is also Chromium-based and runs the same end-to-end test suite. In Firefox, which cannot replace its bookmarks page, the manager opens from a button in the popup, and a few browser features differ. See [Browser support](/browser-support) for the details.
