Bookmark Scout is a free browser extension that puts each page you save in the right bookmark folder. Click its icon, type a few letters of a folder's name, and the page goes there. It works in Chrome today.

<StartHere />

## What you can do with it

- **Save to the right folder**: type part of a folder's name, even a folder deep inside others, and pick it from the list to save the page you are on. See [Your first save](/first-save).
- **Save with one key**: press `Alt+Shift+S` (`⌥⇧S` on a Mac) on any page to save it to the folder that fits it best. A small message in the page says where it went, with **Undo** and other folders to move it to. See [Save with a keyboard shortcut](/guides/save-and-organize#quick-save).
- **Find**: search every bookmark by a word in its title or address, from the toolbar button or the side panel. See [Search and saved searches](/guides/search).
- **Organize**: a bookmarks page that works like Chrome's, with drag and drop, right-click menus, cut, copy, and paste, and undo with `Ctrl+Z` (`Cmd+Z` on a Mac). See [Save and organize](/guides/save-and-organize).
- **Clean up**: remove duplicate bookmarks, tidy addresses full of tracking codes, and find links that no longer work. See [Clean up duplicates and tracking URLs](/guides/clean-up) and [Find and repair dead links](/guides/dead-links).
- **Move your bookmarks**: export them to a file, or bring them in from another browser after a preview. See [Import and export](/guides/import-export).
- **Use AI, only if you want to**: AI is off until you turn it on, and it needs an account with an AI company, which may charge for use. It can suggest a folder or answer questions about your bookmarks, and changes nothing without your approval. See [Set up AI tools](/guides/ai-tools).

## Where your data goes

Bookmark Scout has no account and no server of its own. Your bookmarks stay in your browser. Data leaves the browser only when you use a feature that needs the internet, such as checking for dead links or an AI tool you turned on, and then it goes straight to the website or AI company you chose. Read the [privacy summary](/privacy).

Bookmark Scout is made by an independent developer: no company, no ads, no account, and the code is open source.
